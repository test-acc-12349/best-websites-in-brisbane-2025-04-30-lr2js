Frequently Asked Questions

What is your typical website development timeline?

Most projects are completed within 4-8 weeks, depending on complexity. We follow a structured process: 1 week for planning, 2-3 weeks for design, 2-3 weeks for development, and 1 week for testing and launch.

What type of ongoing support do you provide?

We offer comprehensive maintenance packages including 24/7 monitoring, security updates, content updates, performance optimization, and technical support. Our team is available via phone, email, and live chat.

How much does a website cost?

Website investments typically range from $5,000 to $25,000 depending on your specific requirements. We provide detailed quotes after understanding your needs and objectives during our initial consultation.

Do you handle website hosting and domain registration?

Yes, we provide secure, high-performance hosting solutions and can manage your domain registration and renewal. Our hosting includes daily backups, SSL certificates, and CDN integration.

Can you help with SEO and digital marketing?

Absolutely! We offer comprehensive SEO services and digital marketing strategies to help your website rank better and attract more qualified traffic. This includes keyword research, on-page optimization, and performance tracking.
